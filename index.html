School Secretary

A free desktop study planner for students at any school that uses Brightspace. It keeps what's due, your exams, your classes and your instructors' posts in one place on your computer.

Download School Secretary

Free · about 500 MB · Windows, Mac or Linux · No account with us · For people 16 and older.

School Secretary runs on a computer, not a phone. Open this page on your laptop to download it.

Unofficial. School Secretary is not made by D2L (the company behind Brightspace) and is not made, endorsed or approved by any school. It never completes assignments, labs or quiz answers. It explains the task and points you to your course materials. You still do the work.

How to install

  1. Download the zip for your computer.
  2. Unzip it and open School Secretary.
  3. Enter your school's Brightspace address, then sign in through your school's normal login page.

Opening it the first time

The downloads are not code-signed yet, so your computer shows a warning the first time.

Windows
  1. If your browser says the file "isn't commonly downloaded", choose Keep (in Edge: … → Keep → Keep anyway).
  2. Right-click the zip and choose Extract All. Open SchoolSecretary from the new SchoolSecretary folder, not from inside the zip.
  3. If "Windows protected your PC" appears, click More info.
  4. Click Run anyway.

A black window opens beside your browser. That window is the app, so leave it open. Closing it closes School Secretary.

Mac

For Apple Silicon Macs (M1 or newer). Intel Macs are not supported yet.

  1. Double-click the zip to unzip it, then open SchoolSecretary in the new SchoolSecretary folder. When macOS says it can't check it, click Done.
  2. Go to System Settings → Privacy & Security, scroll down and click Open Anyway, then confirm.
  3. On macOS 14 or older you can instead Control-click the app and choose Open.

A Terminal window opens beside your browser. That window is the app, so leave it open. Closing it closes School Secretary.

Privacy

School Secretary runs on your own computer. You sign in to Brightspace yourself, in a normal browser window, and your course data stays on your computer. Settings has Wipe school data to remove the saved sign-in, the database and cached files. AI features are optional and use your own AI account key.

This page sets no cookies and has no trackers. The full Privacy page is inside the app, under Settings. To ask what we hold about you, to correct it or to delete it, write to schoolsecretary.official@gmail.com.

Our links. Some of our own links to School Secretary (they start with ss-pro-keys.fly.dev/go/) add one to a daily count for that link, then send you straight to our downloads page. We store only the day, the name of the link and a number. We do not store your internet address, your device or a cookie, and we delete the counts after about 13 months. Like any website, our host may see your internet address and keep routine request logs for a short time; we do not use them to find out who you are.

Questions

Is it free?

Yes. School Secretary is free to download and use.

Does it do my homework?

No. It never completes assignments, labs or quiz answers. It explains the task and points you to your course materials.

Is it from my school or from D2L?

No. It is unofficial. Some schools limit automated access or third-party tools, so check your school's acceptable-use and academic-integrity policies.

Which schools does it work with?

Any school that uses Brightspace. You enter your school's Brightspace address when you first open it.

Where does my data go?

It stays on your computer. You can remove it at any time with Wipe school data in Settings.

Why does my computer show a warning?

The downloads are not code-signed yet. The steps above show how to open it.
