Privacy Policy

Last updated 2026-11-19

School Secretary is software you run on your computer. It is not a website that signs in to Brightspace for you in the cloud.

Unofficial: not made by D2L, Brightspace or any school.

In short

  • Your course data stays on your computer. We do not keep a copy of it.
  • Besides your school's own Brightspace site, something leaves only for a feature you turn on: your own AI provider (chat can also send your question to DuckDuckGo for a web search), Google Calendar, a class-times calendar link, the Pro key check or the optional weekly count.
  • Checking for updates asks GitHub for a public file, and the Student aid deadlines card asks our student-aid site, wealthnutz.com, for one. Neither sends your name, school or courses. Like any download, those sites see your internet address.
  • The app never stores your password or two-step codes.
  • Wipe school data (Settings, then System & Privacy) removes your school data from this computer; a few small files stay (see Deleting your data). This app is for people 16 and older.

What stays on this device

  • Your Brightspace cookies and saved sign-in
  • Your school username, so the sign-in box is filled in next time (never your password or two-step codes)
  • Course files, announcements, deadlines, and the local database
  • Calendar files this app writes
  • Your Google Calendar sign-in, only if you connect Google Calendar, in its own file
  • Optional AI keys you paste in Settings
  • Your class-times calendar link, if you paste one (see What can leave this device)
  • Your Pro key, if you enter one
  • Study minutes you log
  • Trial counts: how many review blocks and study packs you made during the Pro trial. Only the numbers, never the content.
  • Which finished courses you chose to hide
  • Your answers to “Was this Update useful?” (Yes, Not really or Skip): the answer, the week and the app version, for your last 50 answers. It also keeps how many Updates have finished (it counts to 3, so it never asks on your first ones), the week it last asked, and whether you chose Don't ask again. Never sent. Settings → Your feedback shows your latest answers, and Delete all there removes all your saved answers. Wipe school data removes all of it.
  • Study coach. The study coach notes which course files you open in the app (a file number and a date) to pick one card a day. It also keeps which cards it showed and what you pressed, for 60 days. If you skip a course’s reading cards, it keeps a skip count for that course and which courses have reading cards switched off, until you bring them back in Settings, turn the coach off and on again, or wipe school data. All of it stays on this computer, is never sent, and is removed by Wipe school data. It stops noting when the coach is off, entries older than about five months are dropped, and Forget this list in Settings clears the file list at any time.
  • Marks and feedback your school has posted, saved during Update so the Marked list works, and the target you type under “What do I need?”. Never sent anywhere. Wipe school data removes them.
  • Feedback notes. When your school posts feedback on an item, the app keeps a short note that it was posted, for up to 7 days. The notes file never holds your mark or the feedback itself (your marks are kept in the local database, listed above), and none of it is sent anywhere. Wipe school data removes these notes.
  • Your choices on the Student aid card (your level and the dates you hid), and whether you chose Don't show again on the calendar-link offer. Never sent. Wipe school data removes them.

Nothing in that list is uploaded to the people who make this app. The one exception is your Pro key, which our key server checks if you use Pro (see What can leave this device).

The only information from the app that we receive on purpose is the weekly usage count below, and only if you turn it on, and the Pro key check described below, and only if you use Pro. Opening the aid card is an ordinary request for a public file, described in the next list.

What can leave this device

  • Your school, when you press Sign in or Update: a browser on this PC talks to Brightspace the same way you would.
  • Your AI provider, only if you add a key: chat, study-plan and practice-question text may be sent to Anthropic or OpenAI under your account. Course files and posts are only sent for courses where you turn Cloud AI on in Settings (it is off until you do). Dates, titles and weights from your schedule can be sent to answer questions about your week.
  • Our Pro key server and Sold through Link, only if you buy or use Pro. When you enter a Pro key, the app sends the key and the name "School Secretary" to our key server to check it. The server gives this computer a random code, and every few days the app sends the key and that code to check it again. No course data goes with it. If this computer is offline, Pro keeps working for up to 14 days after the last check. You pay on a checkout page run by Sold through Link, which handles your card. See "If you buy Pro" below.
  • Google Calendar, only if you connect it: see the Google Calendar section below.
  • Class-times calendar link (optional). If you paste a private calendar link for your class times on the Classes page (from Google, Apple or Outlook), the app saves it on this computer in its own private file. It then asks that calendar's own site for your class times whenever it refreshes, including refreshes it runs by itself. That site can see your internet address. The app uses only your class times, and the rooms if the calendar has them. It keeps a copy of the downloaded calendar file on this computer, so if the link is to a calendar with other events, use one that holds only your classes. That copy is never sent anywhere; Wipe school data deletes it. The link is not sent to us or to an AI service. Anyone with the link can see that calendar. Remove calendar link on the Classes page or Wipe school data deletes the link.
  • Calendar files. The app can make a calendar file on this computer for your own calendar or phone. It holds only due dates, sit-down exams and study times you ask for: the course, the title, and the date and time. It never holds grades, student numbers, passwords or links to your school's portal.
  • A copy on your phone, only if you switch on "Keep a copy on this phone" on the phone page: that phone's browser keeps the next 14 days so you can read them when this computer is off. It holds today's classes, what's overdue or due in the next 14 days, and your next exam: course codes, titles, times and rooms. It never holds grades, student numbers, passwords, links or chat. After 7 days without this computer the phone page stops showing it and erases it the next time it opens, or you can tap Remove saved copy at any time. Turning the phone link off, making a new link, or Wipe school data cannot erase it from the phone. A phone that is no longer paired keeps showing the saved copy until it is 7 days old. To remove it sooner, open More on the phone page and tap Remove saved copy, or clear the site's data in the phone's browser. We never see it.
  • Updates. When the app checks for or downloads a new version, it contacts GitHub, which can see your IP address, as with any download.
  • Our links. Some of our own links to School Secretary (they start with ss-pro-keys.fly.dev/go/) add one to a daily count for that link, then send you straight to our downloads page. We store only the day, the name of the link and a number. We do not store your internet address, your device or a cookie, and we delete the counts after about 13 months. Like any website, our host may see your internet address and keep routine request logs for a short time; we do not use them to find out who you are.
  • Student aid deadlines (WealthNutz). When the Student aid deadlines card on Home is open (it stays open if you left it open), the app downloads one public list of aid and scholarship dates from wealthnutz.com, our free student-aid site. It is the same file for everyone. The request has no name, school, courses, account or province or state in it. Like any web request, it shows wealthnutz.com your internet address and that it came from School Secretary, and the host may keep routine request logs for a short time. We do not use them to find out who you are. The province or state you pick stays on this computer.
  • Web search (only with an AI key). If your saved course files don't answer a question, the app may search the web for it. Your question goes to DuckDuckGo, without your name or course files.
  • Sharing a list. If you share your exams with classmates, the app makes a picture, text or calendar file on this computer with only the exams you tick: the course, the exam name, and the date and time (rooms only on the picture, and only if you turn them on). You send it yourself. It never has grades, student numbers or links to your school's portal. We never see it and we never contact your classmates. Once you send it, the person who gets it can forward it, so only share what you are comfortable with.
  • Weekly usage count (off unless you turn it on; for people 16 and older). After setup, the app asks you once (and once more after Wipe school data). If you close the question without answering, it stays off. If you choose it, once a week the app sends a random code, the app version and the week number. The code is new each week, so weeks cannot be linked together. That is all: no name, email, school, course, deadline, file or computer id. It goes to our own server at wealthnutz.com and is added to a weekly count that keeps no list of codes. We keep the weekly totals for about 13 months. It is never linked to a Pro purchase or a licence. Turn it off in Settings any time and the code on this computer is deleted; Wipe school data deletes it too. Like any website, our host may see your IP address and keep routine request logs for a short time. We do not store it.

Course text is not used to train a School Secretary model. We do not keep a cloud copy of your Brightspace.

If you buy Pro

  • Payment. You pay on a checkout page run by Sold through Link, LLC (an affiliate of Stripe). It takes your card, billing details and email, and the country or postcode it needs to work out tax, under its own privacy policy, which you can reach from the checkout page. School Secretary does not see or save your card number. Stripe's notice of your purchase may reach our key server with details such as your email address; our key server does not save them.
  • What our key server keeps. To make your key work, it keeps a scrambled code made from your key, the Stripe checkout, customer, subscription and payment numbers for your purchase, whether you bought the monthly plan or a term pass, whether the key is active, when it started, and its end date. It also keeps a random code and the date for each computer you activate. It does not keep your name, email address or card number.
  • Your key on the thank-you page. The page that opens after you pay shows your key. Our key server keeps the key itself for 7 days so that page can show it, and after that only the scrambled code. Copy your key and keep it somewhere safe.
  • What the record never contains. Your Brightspace sign-in, courses, grades, files, anything from Google, or the weekly usage code.
  • Our Stripe account. It may show us details of your order, such as your email address and what you bought. We use them only to help you with your purchase.
  • Where our key server is. The app learns the address of our key server from the same public file on our downloads page that it reads to check for updates. That lets us move the key server or switch Pro sales on or off without a new release. The file is the same for everyone and holds nothing about you. Our key server runs in the United States, so if you use Pro, your key and the other items listed above are processed there.
  • Your internet address. Like any website, our key server and its host may see your internet address and keep routine request logs for a short time. We do not store it in the Pro record.
  • How long. We keep the record while your key can still be used. We delete it 12 months after your key stops working, and from our backups within 30 days after that. You can ask us to delete it sooner.
  • Deleting it. Email schoolsecretary.official@gmail.com as described under Contact and your rights. Sold through Link keeps your order and receipt under its own policy; ask Link for those.
  • We do not sell this information and we do not use it for advertising.

Calendar link

Calendar link (optional). If you paste your school's calendar link, the app saves it on this computer in its own private file, because anyone with the link can see your due dates. We never see it. It is not sent to us, to an AI service or to any site other than your school's own. Remove calendar link or Wipe school data deletes it. Sign out and forget this computer does not remove your calendar link: only Remove calendar link or Wipe school data does. If you keep this app's data folder inside a synced or backed-up folder (for example OneDrive, iCloud or Dropbox), that service may copy the link too. Anyone with the link can see your due dates, so if you think it has leaked, remove it here and reset your calendar feed in Brightspace so the old link stops working (if your school does not let you reset it, ask your school's IT help).

The app uses the link to ask your school's own site for your due dates, exam times and course names whenever it updates your school items, including updates it runs by itself. It does not read grades or course files. It keeps the title, date and time, and course for each item, and does not keep the rest of the calendar file.

If you saved a calendar link and there is no working sign-in on this computer (you never signed in, or your sign-in ran out), the app also reads the link by itself once a day while it is running, around the time of its daily update. Each read goes only to your school's own site, the same as pressing Update. If a read fails, the app waits longer before trying again. If the school refuses the link a few times in a row, the app stops reading it until you paste a new one.

Due dates that came from the link are used like any other due date in the app. If you turn on AI help, the title and date of an item can be used there, the same as items from signing in.

Google Calendar (optional, off unless you turn it on)

  • Google Calendar, only if you connect it on the Classes page. School Secretary makes one calendar of its own in your Google account, called “School Secretary”, and writes your due dates, sit-down exams and the study times you plan there: the course, the item name, the date and time, and a short note that School Secretary added it. It sends them when you press Update, and once when you connect; it does not update by itself, so the calendar can be out of date until you press Update. It never writes grades, student numbers, rooms, passwords or links to your school's portal. Google's permission lets it see and change only that one calendar, not your other calendars or events. The connection runs from this computer straight to Google. We have no server in between and never see your Google account or calendar. Your Google sign-in is saved only on this computer, in its own file, and Disconnect or Wipe school data deletes it. When you disconnect you can also delete the “School Secretary” calendar; otherwise Google keeps it, and the events in it, until you delete it there. Google stores that calendar under Google's own privacy policy. If your Google account belongs to your school, your school's rules apply to it too. What School Secretary gets back from Google stays on this computer: it is never sent to an AI provider, never used to train AI, never sold and never shared. School Secretary's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. You can also remove School Secretary's access at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
  • Changes you make in that Google calendar. While Google Calendar is connected, each time the app sends your dates it also reads back the events it put in the “School Secretary” calendar (only their times and its own labels, never events you added yourself) to see if you moved or deleted a study time, and plans around that. On this computer, in its own file, it keeps each study time's course, name and times, and the new time if you moved it. Disconnect, signing out of the app or Wipe school data deletes that file. If you move a due date or exam there, the app puts it back, because due dates come from your school. It never reads your other calendars and never changes anything in your school's site.

What we never do

  • Redistribute another student’s cookies, database, or course files
  • Store your password or two-step codes (you type those in the school’s sign-in window)
  • Read cookie values to display them
  • Run a third-party scrape farm against Brightspace

Deleting your data

Settings → System & Privacy → Wipe school data removes the local database, browser profile, cookies, cached course files, chat history and phone pairing on this computer. It also deletes your Google Calendar sign-in. The “School Secretary” calendar stays in your Google account until you delete it there. Your AI keys can be kept or removed in the same step. Wipe cannot reach a phone. If you kept a copy on your phone, open More on the phone page and tap Remove saved copy, or clear the site's data in the phone's browser.

Wipe also deletes the calendar file the app saved in your Downloads folder (school-secretary.ics), but only while it is still the app's own copy. If you changed that file or saved another file under that name, it stays. Events you already imported into Outlook, Google, Apple or another calendar stay there; delete them in that calendar. The course files the app copies to the SchoolSecretary-Materials folder in Downloads also stay, including the Study pack folder; delete that folder yourself if you no longer want them. Files you downloaded or moved yourself, like an Export file, are not touched.

Wipe keeps a few small files so the app still opens and you don't lose what you paid for: your settings, a random install code, your Pro key, the date your free trial started, and the date a term pass was first entered (stored as a scrambled code, not the key). Wipe school data clears the trial counts. It does not give a second trial. To remove the Pro key, use Move key to another computer in Settings. Wipe school data does not delete our Pro record. Move key to another computer frees the key on our key server, and an email to us deletes the record (see If you buy Pro).

Contact and your rights

The person in charge of protecting personal information for School Secretary is its owner. Write to schoolsecretary.official@gmail.com to ask what we hold about you, to correct it or to delete it. We reply within 30 days. Almost everything stays on your PC. A phone copy, if you turned it on, stays on that phone. We hold neither. If you use Pro, we hold the small Pro record described under If you buy Pro. Settings → System & Privacy → Wipe school data deletes your school data (see what it keeps above). If you turned on the weekly usage count, turning it off deletes your code on this computer. Our count keeps no list of codes, so nothing on our side points back to you. If you bought Pro, Sold through Link keeps your order, payment and receipt under its own privacy policy, which you can reach from the checkout page. To have our Pro record deleted, email us your Pro key, or the email address and date on your Link receipt, and we will delete it, and remove it from our backups within 30 days. The key then stops working.
